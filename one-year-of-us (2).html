From my senior... to my love, Jithin.

Dear Jithin,

I know I'm not the same person you met back then. I have changed a lot. Maybe I have changed more than I even realize. But one thing that is still here, exactly where it has always been, is my love for you.

Thank you for giving me so many beautiful moments in my life. Every small thing you did was special to me — even the things you probably thought were nothing.

I still remember our first fight, our first kiss, our first outing to Trichy, and our first movie. Whenever I think about them, I still feel shy and smile to myself. Thank you for giving me memories that feel like little pieces of happiness I can carry forever.

We have fought each other so many times, but somehow every time we came back a little closer. Maybe that's what makes our story ours. It hasn't been perfect. It has been real.

Now things are different. You have graduated. I'm still here in this college. And long distance is difficult. I won't pretend that it isn't. But I want us to overcome it together.

I don't know exactly what every tomorrow will look like, but I know what I want: I want to see this story reach a beautiful, successful ending — not because our story has to end, but because I want to see us make it through every difficult chapter and still choose each other.

Thank you for being my senior, my friend, my favourite person, and my love. Thank you for every laugh, every fight, every hug, every memory and every little moment that became a part of me.

My senior became my love. And somehow, my love became my home.

Love you, Jithin. ❤️
